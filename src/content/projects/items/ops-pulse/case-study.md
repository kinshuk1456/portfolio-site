# Ops Pulse — Case Study

## Context
Weekly operations reviews were slow and inconsistent. Different teams showed different numbers, and a lot of time was spent *finding* the truth instead of deciding what to do.

## Problem
- Metrics were scattered across spreadsheets and ad-hoc queries
- KPI definitions weren’t consistent
- Reporting refreshes were manual and error-prone

## Approach
1. **Define the decision moments** (what questions leaders ask every week)
2. **Choose a small KPI set** tied to action (throughput, backlog, service level, cycle time)
3. **Standardize definitions** and document assumptions
4. **Design for the meeting**: a top-down narrative (health → drivers → drill-down)

## Outcome
- Faster weekly reviews
- Less time spent reconciling numbers
- Clearer prioritization because everyone looked at the same metrics

## What I’d do next
Add lightweight instrumentation for leading indicators and a “what changed” view to surface anomalies proactively.
