# Operations KPI Dashboard (Power BI) — Case Study

## Context
During my Data Analytics Internship at PIM Brands, operational KPI visibility depended on fragmented views across tools. Stakeholders needed a repeatable, decision-ready view.

## Problem
- KPIs were difficult to monitor consistently across teams
- Data lived across multiple sources (including Snowflake and Redzone)
- Workflow inefficiencies were hard to spot quickly

## Approach
- Gathered KPI requirements with engineering and operations stakeholders
- Built repeatable SQL queries and views to support consistent reporting
- Designed Power BI dashboards to support ongoing operational reviews (health → drivers → drill-down)

## Outcome
Improved operational KPI visibility and supported faster data-driven decision making without changing existing systems.

## Notes
Where appropriate, I kept definitions documented to reduce metric debates and improve trust.
