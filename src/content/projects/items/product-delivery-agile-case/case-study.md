# Product Delivery & Agile Execution Case — Case Study

## Context
In my Software Developer role at Fly Me High, I worked close to delivery: turning stakeholder inputs into actionable work, keeping QA/UAT aligned, and supporting release readiness.

## Problem
- Vague requirements created churn and rework
- QA/UAT alignment happened too late
- Releases needed clearer communication and readiness checks

## Approach
- Requirements gathering → user stories → acceptance criteria
- Backlog grooming and sprint planning support
- QA coordination and UAT readiness alignment
- Release communication and documentation to keep stakeholders informed

## Outcome
A more predictable delivery rhythm, improved release readiness, and faster deployments (cycle time reduced by ~40% during the role).
