# Trail Notes: My Reset Routine

When my weeks get overloaded, I don’t try to “power through” — I reset the system.

## The loop
- **Walk first** (no headphones if possible)
- **Lift** (keep it short, keep it consistent)
- **Write** (what matters this week?)
- **Plan** (3 priorities, no more)

## Why it works
It’s not about discipline. It’s about reducing noise so the signal comes back.

If it can’t fit on one page, it isn’t clear yet.
