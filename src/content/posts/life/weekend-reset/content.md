# Weekend Reset

When work is intense, I try to keep the reset ritual boring on purpose.

- Long walk (no podcasts)
- Lift (simple progression)
- 30 minutes: clean inbox + calendar

## Why it works
- Movement clears the mental cache
- A short review prevents decision debt
- Monday feels lighter

## Tiny rule
If the plan takes more than 5 minutes to write, it’s too complicated.
