# Translating Stakeholder Needs into Requirements

Stakeholders rarely show up with “requirements.” They show up with:
- pain
- urgency
- opinions
- partial context

The job is to turn that into something a team can ship.

## The flow I use
### 1) Start with the job-to-be-done
What are they trying to accomplish? What does “success” look like?

### 2) Capture constraints
Time, system limitations, compliance, dependencies.

### 3) Define acceptance criteria early
If it can’t be tested, it isn’t clear.

### 4) Document assumptions
Assumptions become bugs later.

## Output artifacts
- A short PRD (problem, users, scope, non-goals)
- User stories with acceptance criteria
- A release note template (what changed, who it impacts)

## Closing
Good requirements aren’t long—they’re *testable* and *shared*.
